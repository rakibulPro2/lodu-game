LUDO

The classic board game

Game setup

Red0/4
Green0/4
Yellow0/4
Blue0/4

Winner!

    How to play

    • Every player has his own dice next to his corner. Tap your dice on your turn. Other dice stay locked.
    • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its yard.
    • Move a token the number shown on the dice and follow the track clockwise.
    • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to its yard. Tokens on star or start squares are safe.
    • Rolling a 6, capturing a token, or reaching home gives you another turn.
    • Three sixes in a row ends your turn.
    • Get all 4 tokens into the center to win.
    • Team Up: Red & Yellow play against Green & Blue. Partners never capture each other. A team wins when all 8 tokens are home. Once you have finished, your dice moves your partner's tokens.
